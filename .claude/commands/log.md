---
description: The site diary. Weather, labour on site, instructions, visits, delays, promises. In an extension of time claim it is the evidence.
---

1. Take what the operator says and write it as one dated entry: `npm run construction -- log <project> "<what happened>" [--weather= --workers= --company=]`.
2. Keep the operator's facts, add nothing. Names, times, instruction numbers and quantities stay exactly as given.
3. If the entry mentions an instruction, a delay or a defect, offer the matching record (variation, delay, defect) in one line.
4. Read it back: `npm run construction -- diary <project> --days=14`.
